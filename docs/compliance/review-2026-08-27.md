# Compliance review — product programme (2026-08-27)

Reviewer: compliance-reviewer agent (not legal advice). Scope: command of
record, packages.md, scanner-suite.md, secrets UI/registry, executor, data
flow, MASTER_PLAN T-081..T-093, the one campaign report. Each item ends
with the question to take to counsel.

## Blocking (before the referenced task ships)
1. **Tenant access to the exchange-credential vault (T-081).** Once tenancy
   exists, a tenant admin storing exchange keys in our DB makes us a
   custodian of client credentials even if never read. Required: exchange
   group operator-only (platform-admin RBAC; hidden + 403 for tenant
   roles) with a test. Counsel: does storing encrypted, unread client keys
   create custody/agency or controller obligations?
2. **`docs/decisions/` did not exist for gate (c) (T-095).** Created with a
   template (jurisdiction, licence/exemption, counsel, date, scope).
   Counsel: licences for (i) own-funds and (ii) client-funds execution.
3. **No legal pages (T-085).** Terms, privacy, risk disclosure, refund,
   cookies, affiliate terms, AUP, DPA, subprocessors, company identity —
   drafts required; risk disclosure accepted at sign-up (versioned, with
   timestamp/IP) and linked from every spread/PnL surface. Counsel: which
   are mandatory per jurisdiction; is click-wrap sufficient?
4. **Case studies with zero evidence (T-085/086).** The only report shows
   zero executed cycles: every performance figure today would be
   invented. Required: copy hard-gated on a report path; a build lint
   failing on any %/currency figure outside a `docs/campaigns/<id>`
   citation. Counsel: is a labelled simulated case study a financial
   promotion (UK/EU)?

## High
5. Simulated results need a mandated hypothetical-performance disclaimer
   (CFTC 4.41-style) on console, exports, API, alerts, reports, with the
   model's exclusions (no transfers, top-of-book fills, unknown
   withdrawal status, 1× perps with hard stop) in the footer.
6. "Arbitrage/carry/harvest" framing implies riskless profit; risk
   disclosure must state untradeable spreads, liquidation/counterparty
   risk, exchange failure, fees-only modelling; ban "harvest", "passive
   income", "risk-free", "guaranteed" via copy lint.
7. Sized, per-venue alerts may be "advice" not "data": outputs are
   measurements/filters the user configured; no buy/sell verbs; no
   default asset-picking rule templates. Counsel: MiCA CASP advice, UK
   RAO, VARA advisory, US IA/CTA boundaries.
8. Perps/funding pages promote derivatives to retail: UK retail crypto
   derivatives banned (PS20/10), US offshore perps unavailable, EU MiFID.
   Required: country capture at sign-up, geo/self-declaration gating or
   per-row "information only" notice.
9. GDPR: no Art. 30 data map; audit rows carry identities and are never
   deleted. Required before T-081 prod: data map, lawful basis and
   retention per dataset, erasure by pseudonymising audit identities.
10. Telegram chat IDs are personal data sent to a third-country processor:
    encrypt at rest, never log (push.go logs them on failure), delete with
    rule/org, list Telegram as subprocessor with transfer mechanism.
11. Refund policy vs EU/UK 14-day withdrawal right: express consent to
    immediate performance at checkout; B2B/consumer status at sign-up;
    reconcile with Paddle buyer terms; disclose trial demotion/purge.
12. Affiliates: sanctions screening at enrolment and payout, pre-approval
    of promotional material, prohibited-claims list, W-8/W-9, DAC7/1099
    reporting, clawback; UK promotion-approval liability.

## Medium
13. Watch-tier "live numbers" on the marketing site are measurements, not
    campaign figures — label as a live screener sample or omit.
14. SecretsSection copy said exchange keys "fall back to env" (they have
    no env fallback) — fixed; exchange table should be ADMIN-view only.
15. Stored keys still create breach liability: separate KMS key + rotation
    for the exchange group, excluded from routine backups, 72 h breach
    runbook; "read-only key" is a recommendation we cannot verify.
16. "Beyond the gate": client-funds execution implies trade-scope keys,
    execution-agent/broker role, licences (VARA, MiCA execution, FCA,
    US), client agreements, segregation, E&O/cyber insurance — add to the
    command as prerequisites beyond (a)–(d).
17. UAE: VARA "Advisory Services" and marketing regulation; ADGM/DIFC/SCA.
18. EU: MiCA advice boundary; DSA/UCPD for affiliates and auto-renewal.
19. UK: cryptoasset financial-promotion regime (approver, risk warnings,
    24 h cooling-off); possible geo-blocking.
20. US: CTA/CPO for perps advice, IA status, FTC endorsement guides,
    state auto-renewal laws, CCPA/CPRA.
21. Large default paper balances make trial PnL look like returns: show
    bps/percent first, label "simulated balance".
22. Retention tiers vs GDPR portability: free personal-data export
    separate from the paid data-export entitlement.

## Low
23. `entitlements_override` must be schema-validated after merge so
    `execution.live` can never become true; add a test (T-082).
24. Competitor prices/names stay internal; no comparative claims publicly.
25. Support SLAs are contractual: SLA annex with service credits; exclude
    third-party venue data from uptime commitments.

Live execution is correctly impossible in code today
(`internal/execution/executor.go`, `ErrLiveTradingDisabled`); no finding
requires touching it.
