# Affiliate Programme Terms

**Version:** [n] · **Effective:** [date]

These terms govern participation in the {{brand}} affiliate programme run
by {{company}}. They sit alongside the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and
the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 1. Eligibility

- Any organisation on a paid package, or a publisher we approve.
- You must provide your legal name, country, payout details and the tax
  form we request (for example W-8 or W-9 where applicable) [COUNSEL: DAC7
  and 1099 reporting thresholds; item 12].
- We screen applicants and payees against sanctions lists at enrolment and
  before each payout. Residents of sanctioned jurisdictions may not join.
- We may decline or remove any affiliate at our discretion, with notice.

## 2. Commission

- **First 12 months** of a referred organisation's paid subscriptions:
  {{aff.rate_year1}} of net revenue.
- **Thereafter**, while both accounts remain active: {{aff.rate_after}}.
- **Institution contracts**: {{aff.rate_institution}}, first year only.
- "Net revenue" means the amount our payment processor reports after tax,
  processor fees and refunds. Commission is computed in decimal arithmetic
  and reverses on refund or chargeback.

## 3. Attribution

- Referral by link or code. **Last click wins.** Attribution storage lasts
  45 days from the click.
- An organisation is bound to its referrer at the moment the organisation
  is created, not at first visit.
- We do not re-attribute existing customers.

## 4. Maturation and payout

- Commission on a payment becomes payable 45 days after the payment
  cleared, to cover the money-back window and typical chargeback periods.
- Payouts are made monthly on the 15th for the previous calendar month
  once your matured balance reaches {{aff.threshold}}.
- Payout by bank transfer or PayPal through our processor's payout
  facility. No crypto payouts.
- Your dashboard shows clicks, sign-ups, trials, conversions and accrued,
  matured and paid amounts. Every figure is read from the ledger; nothing
  is estimated.

## 5. Prohibited conduct

Any of the following voids the affected commission and may end your
participation:

1. **Self-referral** — the same person, card, payout account or
   organisation domain on both sides.
2. **Coupon, brand-bid or trademark traffic** — bidding on our name,
   registering domains containing it, or coupon-site placements.
3. **Incentivised sign-ups** — cash-back or rewards to the customer.
4. **Performance claims.** Your material may not state or imply any return,
   hit rate, spread size or income. You may quote a published report from
   our campaign archive **verbatim, with its fees and verdict**, or say
   nothing about performance. The words "guaranteed", "risk-free" and
   "passive income", and any invented figure, end your participation
   immediately.
5. **Impersonation** — presenting yourself as {{brand}} or {{company}}, or
   using our logo outside the asset kit.
6. **Spam** — unsolicited messages, forum floods, fake reviews.
7. **Misleading comparisons** — comparative claims against other products.

A referral base with a refund or chargeback rate we consider abnormal over
any 90-day window is placed on manual review and payouts are held.

## 6. Pre-approval and disclosure

- Promotional material that describes the product's results or the risk of
  trading must be **submitted to us before publication** [COUNSEL: item 19 —
  UK financial-promotion approval liability; item 18 — EU DSA/UCPD].
- You must clearly disclose the affiliate relationship wherever you place a
  link, in line with FTC, ASA and equivalent guidance [COUNSEL: item 20].
- You must include the sentence "{{brand}} is a market-data and simulation
  tool. It does not trade, hold funds or give advice." wherever you describe
  the product.

## 7. Clawback

We may recover paid commission for up to 180 days after payment where fraud
or a breach of §5 is established, by set-off against future payouts or by
invoice.

## 8. Term, changes, termination

Either side may end participation with 30 days' notice; matured commission
is paid, unmatured commission continues to mature unless §5 applies. We may
change rates for future periods with 30 days' notice.

## 9. Relationship

You are an independent contractor, not our agent, partner or employee. You
may not make commitments on our behalf.
