## 2. Your choices

- The consent banner appears on first visit. Analytics and attribution
  storage are set only after you accept.
- Change or withdraw consent at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in
  the footer or from console settings.
- Browser controls can block or delete cookies; strictly necessary cookies
  are needed to sign in.
