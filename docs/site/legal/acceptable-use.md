# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** [n] · **Effective:** [date]

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It describes
what you may and may not do with {{brand}}.

## 1. You may

- Configure rules, templates and thresholds for your own analysis.
- Receive alerts on the channels your package includes.
- Run simulated ("paper") execution and study the ledgers and reports.
- Export data within your package's history depth for your own use.
- Use the client API within its rate limits and scopes.
- Share screenshots and exports, provided the "simulated" labelling and
  the risk footer remain visible.

## 2. You may not

**Circumvention**
- Bypass or probe package limits, rate limits or role restrictions.
- Share one seat between several people, or share API keys.
- Run more than one trial per organisation, or create organisations to
  obtain repeated trials.

**Data**
- Scrape the website, console or API beyond documented endpoints and
  limits.
- Redistribute market data, spreads or alerts as a feed, signal service or
  channel to people outside your organisation.
- Resell, sublicense or white-label the Service without a written
  agreement.

**Misrepresentation**
- Present simulated results as real trading results or as a track record.
- Remove the "simulated" label or the risk footer from any export, alert
  or screenshot you publish.
- Claim that {{brand}} trades, advises, or promises any outcome.
- Use our name or marks to promote a trading service, fund or signal group.

**Security and integrity**
- Attempt unauthorised access to any account, system or data.
- Introduce malware or interfere with the Service or other users.
- Use the Service to break a venue's terms, evade a venue's restrictions or
  manipulate any market.

**Legal**
- Use the Service from a jurisdiction where we do not provide it, or to
  violate sanctions, anti-money-laundering or any other law.
- Use the Service to provide regulated investment services to others
  without the licences those services require.

## 3. Alerts are yours

Alerts report conditions you set. We do not supply default rules that pick
assets for you, and an alert is not a recommendation. If you forward alerts
to other people, you are responsible for how they are understood and for
keeping the simulated label and risk footer attached.

## 4. Enforcement

We may pause rules, downgrade channels, suspend API keys or suspend the
organisation for breaches, proportionate to the harm, and will tell you
what we did and why. Repeated or serious breaches end the contract under
the Terms. We may report unlawful activity to authorities.

## 5. Reporting

Report abuse or security issues to [abuse e-mail] / [security e-mail]. We
acknowledge security reports within [n] business days.
